# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

IT Learn is committed to maintaining the security and integrity of its systems, services, and user data. If you discover a security vulnerability, we encourage you to report it responsibly.

Security reports can be submitted to:

**[security@itlearn.be](mailto:security@itlearn.be)**

Please include:

* A clear description of the vulnerability
* Steps required to reproduce the issue
* The affected system, service, or webpage
* Any relevant screenshots, logs, or supporting information

## Responsible Disclosure

If you discover a vulnerability affecting IT Learn, you must:

* Act in good faith and with the sole purpose of identifying and reporting the vulnerability.
* Limit testing to the minimum actions necessary to verify the existence of the issue.
* Immediately cease testing after obtaining sufficient evidence.
* Report the vulnerability to IT Learn as soon as reasonably possible.

You must not:

* Access, modify, copy, disclose, or delete data that does not belong to you.
* Attempt to gain unauthorized or persistent access to any system.
* Disrupt, degrade, or interfere with the availability of IT Learn services.
* Share, publish, sell, or otherwise disclose information regarding the vulnerability before IT Learn has confirmed that the issue has been fully resolved.
* Use the vulnerability for personal gain or to cause harm to IT Learn, its users, customers, partners, or third parties.

All information relating to a discovered vulnerability must be treated as confidential until IT Learn provides written authorization for public disclosure.

## Legal Notice

This policy does not grant permission to access, test, scan, or interact with IT Learn systems beyond what is authorized by applicable law.

Unauthorized access to systems, misuse of vulnerabilities, disclosure of confidential information, or actions causing damage to IT Learn or third parties may constitute violations of applicable laws and may result in civil and/or criminal proceedings.

## Our Commitment

IT Learn appreciates responsible security research and will review all legitimate vulnerability reports. We will investigate reported issues and take appropriate action where necessary to protect our users and services.
